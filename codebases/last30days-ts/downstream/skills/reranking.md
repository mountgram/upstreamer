# Reranking Guidance

Use this guidance when reviewing or adjusting ranked evidence from Last30Days TS.

- Favor recent items inside the requested lookback window, but keep older context only when it explains why a current discussion matters.
- Raise items with clear engagement: upvotes, comments, likes, views, stars, market volume, or repeated discussion across independent sources.
- De-duplicate by story, not by exact title. If Reddit, YouTube, and web coverage describe the same event, group them as one cluster.
- Prefer first-hand or platform-native evidence over summaries of summaries.
- Penalize generic SEO pages, thin snippets, and results that only match a common word in the topic.
- When two items tie, choose the one with better source diversity, clearer date, and a citation that a reader can inspect.
